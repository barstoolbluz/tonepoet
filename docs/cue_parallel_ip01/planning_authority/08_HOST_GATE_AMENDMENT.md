# Host gate amendment — IP-06 ReplayGain attribution

**Date:** 2026-10-22. **Origin:** TonePoet build host, after review of the R2 corrective
planning bundle against the baseline source. **Scope:** IP-06's decision procedure only.
**Not changed:** the architecture, the S1/S3 selection, packet IDs, packet scopes, the
dependency DAG, and every packet from IP-01 through IP-05. No production source edited.

## The defect in the gate

S1's direct CUE route can compute ReplayGain during the decode it is already performing,
when the source-pass eligibility predicate holds. The predicate was confirmed present in
the baseline (`cue_source_replaygain_eligible`, `src/convert/pipeline/stages.rs`): it
requires ReplayGain enabled, no merge, a lossless PCM target, non-hybrid WavPack, a
trusted inherited-tag policy, and every track a mono or stereo `CueStreamSegment`.

An S3 worker decoding its own range cannot reuse that shared sequential mirror. Under the
Convert UI default of `Both`, the initial pilot therefore pays a post-output ReplayGain
scan that S1 does not. That is a cost of the pilot's deferred observer, not a cost of
ranged decoding or parallel encoding.

As written, IP-06 would have measured the two transports under unequal ReplayGain
treatment and could have recorded the resulting wall-clock loss as evidence against S3 —
rejecting a design that may qualify once the observer exists. The bundle already named the
observer as a conditional follow-on, but nothing in the gate required attributing the scan
before reaching a verdict.

## What the amendment requires

1. **Observe, do not infer, which path S1 took.** Each run records whether S1 actually
   used source-pass analysis or the post-output scan.
2. **Matched RG `Off` and `Both` cells** for every primary comparison, sharing a
   configuration SHA-256 with their pair.
3. **Attribute the scan.** S3's post-output ReplayGain scan is reported as its own
   measured interval, and the RG-Off and RG-Both speedups are reported separately rather
   than blended.
4. **A third verdict, `deferred`.** When S3 shows a meaningful advantage under RG `Off`
   but loses under `Both` primarily because of that scan, the pilot is preserved opt-in
   with S1 default, the `PX-C` source-pass ReplayGain observer is promoted into one
   focused packet reusing the existing native measurement and album-reduction machinery,
   and IP-06's qualification is repeated on the result before any final verdict.

The observer is not to be built speculatively, and S3 is not promoted without demonstrated
end-to-end improvement with ReplayGain `Both`.

## Verified baseline claims

The amendment rests on claims checked directly against commit `98aae2b`, not on the
planning bundle's own assertions: the source-pass predicate above; `ToolRunner::run_pipeline`
and the segmented runner refusing non-Unix with `Unsupported`; the direct decoder command
carrying no seek; the segmented transport advancing one consumer at a time; tool-family
permits collapsing to one per family through a set, so a two-child S3 worker would
undercount; the Convert UI ReplayGain pill selecting `Both` as its first option; and
adjacent `CueStreamSegment`s coalescing into a single scheduled work unit.
